How it works

From PDF to cited answer in three steps

01

Ingest

PyMuPDF extracts per-page text and detects sections. Pages are chunked while keeping source, page, and section on every chunk.

02

Retrieve

ChromaDB stores embeddings. MMR retrieval pulls diverse, relevant chunks across pages and documents for each question.

03

Cite

The model answers only from retrieved context, tagging each claim with its source and page. Quotes are verbatim spans.

Capabilities

Built for academic verification

Section-aware chunking

Chunks carry section, page, and source so citations point to the exact location in the original PDF.

Page-number citations

Every claim cites a filename and page, with the verbatim quote shown alongside it for verification.

Conversation memory

Follow-up questions are rewritten into standalone queries using prior turns, so retrieval stays accurate.

Grounded answers

The model answers strictly from context and says when it does not know, rather than inventing sources.
